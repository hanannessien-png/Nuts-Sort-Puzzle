FULL DESCRIPTION (2683/4000 characters)
Turn a mixed stack into a perfect color match in Screw Sort Master. Move colorful nuts between bolts, plan your next move and finish the board before time runs out. This nuts and bolts puzzle combines easy tap controls with a timed sorting challenge.

HOW TO PLAY
Tap a bolt to select its top nut, then tap another bolt with space to move it. Only the top nut can move, so the order of your choices matters. Group each color on its own bolt to solve the puzzle before the countdown ends.

An empty bolt can make all the difference. Use spare space to uncover the color you need, rearrange a tricky stack and keep your next move in mind. Simple rules make it easy to start, while more colors and tougher layouts give you more to think about.

A COLOR SORT CHALLENGE WITH A TIMER
Every level is a race to organize the board. Balance careful planning with quick decisions as you work through mixed stacks of nuts. Keep an eye on the clock, avoid unnecessary moves and find a cleaner route to the solution.

EARN STARS AND IMPROVE YOUR BEST
Complete levels to earn stars and coins, unlock the next challenge and build your progress across the level map. Replay completed puzzles to improve your move count and aim for a five-star result. Your choices matter, not just your speed.

HELP WHEN YOU NEED IT
Use Hint to reveal a next move, Undo to take back your last move, or Extra Bolt to add more space to the board. Spend earned coins on power-up packs in the shop. Optional rewarded ads can also provide in-game rewards when available.

MAKE THE WORKSHOP YOURS
Unlock themes, nut finishes, bolts and bases with in-game coins. Mix and match your favorites to give your puzzle board a different look as you progress.

RETURN FOR DAILY REWARDS
Collect daily coin rewards and build your streak toward weekly and monthly bonuses. Put those coins toward a new style or save them for help with a difficult board.

SMALL DETAILS THAT HELP YOU PLAY
- Tap controls for moving one nut at a time.
- A visible timer and move counter during each puzzle.
- Hints, undo moves and extra bolts for tricky situations.
- Optional color labels to help distinguish the nuts.
- Adjustable sound and vibration settings.
- Progress saved on your device, with no account required.
