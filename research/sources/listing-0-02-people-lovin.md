🧩 Simple to play, relaxing to master

Enjoy over 1000+ levels of colorful nuts and bolts. Each level is designed to be easy to pick up but offers gradually increasing difficulty to keep your mind active. Sort the same color nuts, plan, and discover the satisfying feeling of completing each bolt nut combination. With no time limits, you can sort at your own pace, clear your thoughts, and enjoy a stress-free color match game.

Whether you want to pass the time or sharpen your mind, this engaging color sort puzzle game is ideal. Simple controls make it easy to move nuts, switch between bolts, and organize them into a perfect color stack. The game improves concentration, promotes mental calmness, and is suitable for all ages.

🔧 How to Play:

1\. Tap to move a nut from one screw to another

2\. Sort all nuts by color to complete each level

3\. Plan ahead – some nuts may block others

4\. Restart freely – no time limit, no pressure
