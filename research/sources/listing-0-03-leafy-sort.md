**Screw It... or Solve It?**

Welcome to **Nut Sort - Color Sort Puzzle**, a world where every bolt is a brain teaser. Your mission? Unscrew bolts, sort them by color, and stack them into perfect columns of 4. Sounds simple? Think again!

With each level, the puzzles get trickier, the mechanics more twisted, and the satisfaction? Oh yes, it gets better and better.

**Game Features**

👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 Made for all ages and skill levels

👆 One-finger control: simple to play, easy to enjoy

🔊 ASMR sounds for a relaxing experience

🧠 Logic puzzles that get more challenging with every level

💥 Super satisfying physics: every move feels just right

🏝️ Discover & unlock beautiful islands with calming themes

🎨 Minimal yet polished design for smooth gameplay

**How To Play**

⚙︎» Unscrew bolts and move them into the new columns

⚙︎» Match 4 of the same color bolts in a column to collect them

⚙︎» Use booster items to make levels easier to complete

⚙︎» Earn bolts after every level to unlock new islands!

**Why It’s So Hard to Put Down**

⏣ Sharpen your logical thinking without it feeling like a chore

⏣ Perfect for daily brain workouts or stress relief

⏣ Builds focus, patience, and sequencing skills

⏣ Keeps your brain engaged and your hands busy

Collect bolts, unlock mysterious islands, and put your logic to the test. No matter how long you play, every level brings a fresh twist for your brain.
