# Screw Sort Master: Google Play copy research
Research date: 2026-11-05. Depth: focused Quick research. Sources consulted: 10 selected sources.

## Executive summary
Use original, readable copy centered on color sort puzzle, nuts and bolts, sorting, and timed challenges. These terms match both the product and the vocabulary in direct competitor descriptions. Their presence in competitors is evidence of category wording, not evidence of search volume or a causal ranking advantage. [[1]](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.inspiredsquare.nut.sort) [[2]](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=game.nuts.nutsort.watersort.color.puzzles) [[3]](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.leaf.nutsort.color.puzzles)

## Background and comparison
Inspired Square emphasizes color matching and bolt space. People Lovin emphasizes relaxing sorting and explicitly promises no timer pressure. Leafy describes matching four pieces and collecting them. Those differences mean competitor descriptions cannot simply be reused for this game. [[1]](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.inspiredsquare.nut.sort) [[2]](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=game.nuts.nutsort.watersort.color.puzzles) [[3]](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.leaf.nutsort.color.puzzles)

WeMaster describes screw removal and freeing plates; Tripledot's Nut Sort describes matching-color restrictions; Tumb's liquid game describes pouring between bottles. None of these mechanics should be imported into Screw Sort Master's listing. The current game moves the top nut to any bolt with available capacity, and has a countdown. [[4]](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.tangle.nuts.bolts) [[5]](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/nut-sort-color-sorting-game/id6476144780) [[6]](https://www.tumbgames.com/games/liquid-sort-puzzle)

## Rules and analysis
Google sets maxima of 80 and 4,000 characters, not required target lengths. Google asks for clear, accurate descriptions and warns against repetitive or irrelevant metadata. A natural description shorter than the maximum is preferable to padding it with keywords. [[7]](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/13393723) [[8]](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9898842)

Official discovery guidance stresses relevant, high-quality experiences. We have no evidence that a description alone guarantees first position, or that any fixed keyword density is optimal. [[9]](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9958766?hl=en-GB)

## Recommendations
The supplied short description is 78 characters. The full description is 2,683 characters including line breaks. It highlights timed sorting, stars, replay, power-ups, cosmetics, daily rewards and accessibility settings, verified against the current source code. Avoid no-timer, ad-free, unlimited lives, thousands of levels and cognitive-health claims. Do not use competitor names in the listing.

After launch, test alternate descriptions with localized store listing experiments, changing one asset at a time. This evaluates conversion, not a guaranteed search-ranking boost. [[10]](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/6227309)

## Limitations
Publisher listings are primary evidence of advertised wording only (Tier 3), not independent proof of benefits or market rank. Google documentation is authoritative for Google's own rules (Tier 1-equivalent). No paid keyword-volume tool, rank tracking, competitor gameplay test or controlled conversion test was used. No current ranking or download-count claims are made. Dates of description edits are unavailable. Exact algorithm weights remain unknown.
